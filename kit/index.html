The wordmark

It is type. There is no symbol.

No monogram, no lockup, and nothing to trace. Two arrangements and two colourways cover every placement we could think of.

  • Use the one line form wherever it fits. Use the stacked form below about 200 pixels of width.
  • White goes on Blue #00142D or on a dark photograph. Ink goes on white, on the tint, or on any pale ground.
  • Leave the height of one capital letter clear on all four sides. The point is that nothing crowds it, not that it is measured.
  • SVG for anything on a screen. PNG for anything that will not take an SVG. The letters in the SVG are outlines, so it does not need the font installed.
  • Do not retype it. The tracking is 0.13em and it will not match by eye.
  • Do not recolour it, outline it, add a shadow, rotate it, or set it on an angle.
  • Do not put it inside a box, a circle, or a badge of your own. There is a badge above if you want one.
  • Do not use the sun mark in the browser tab as an Alliance logo. That is Bodhi's, and there is no Alliance symbol.

Colour

Three rules, and they are not stylistic.

  • Yellow #FFD110Buttons only.
  • Salmon #FF6F47Hairlines, numerals, links, hovers.
  • Blue #00142DThe ink.
  • Body grey #455E77Body copy on light.
  • Accent text #BE4319Red type under 24 pixels.
  • Tint #F2F5F7The second light ground.
  • Cream #FFFCF5The page ground.
  • The gradient162 degrees through #ADC4E0, #C0D1E7, #DEDDE8, #FFC7B8, and #FFF0B2.
  • Yellow is only ever a button. Not a background, not a border, and not a heading.
  • Salmon is only ever a hairline, a numeral, a link, or a hover state. It measures 2.7:1 on a light ground and fails accessibility as text. For red type under 24 pixels use #BE4319, which measures 5.24:1 on white.
  • Blue is the ink. #455E77 is the body grey. Both were set from measured contrast, so neither gets lightened to taste.
  • Type on a gradient ground goes in Blue. The blue end of the ramp is darker than it looks, so keep anything smaller than 24 pixels off it.

Type, texture, and the rest of it

Four ingredients, recombined.

A light gradient ground, hairline circle geometry, film grain, and Montserrat. If a layout seems to need a fifth, it does not.

  • Montserrat, weights 400, 500, 600, and 700. Free from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License, which ships in the kit.
  • The grain is a fractal noise tile at 0.20 opacity, multiplied. The usable range is 0.18 to 0.24. Below that it vanishes, above it the page looks dirty. It is already in every ground, so do not add a second layer.
  • The rings only ever sit on the gradient. Never behind a logo, a card, a table, or a photograph. They are atmosphere, not a pattern, which is why the kit does not ship them loose.
  • No illustration, no photography, no icon sets, and no stock imagery. If a graphic looks empty, the answer is space and type.
  • Do not stretch a ground to a size it is not, and do not add a colour overlay, a duotone, or a blur.

What you can say

The two nouns, and the claims.

  • You are an Alliance partner. An Alliance installer is an independent installer approved to offer Climate First Bank loans through OneEthos. The two are not interchangeable, and neither one is a member.
  • All six of you were in at the start, so founding Alliance partner is accurate. The badge above says exactly that.
  • The Strong Tail Solar Alliance is operated by OneEthos, a technology subsidiary of Climate First Corp. Include that wherever you describe what the Alliance is.
  • Link to strongtail.bodhi.solar, or to your own partner page under /partners/.
  • State your number. Do not call it a discount, savings, or a deal.
  • The Alliance lists your terms. It does not endorse your product.
  • Do not use another partner's logo without asking them. This kit carries Alliance material only, on purpose.
  • Do not put your own logo on an Alliance asset from this kit. If you want a co-branded graphic, ask and we will make one.
  • Serial commas, and no em dashes. A sentence with a number in it beats a sentence with an adjective in it.
