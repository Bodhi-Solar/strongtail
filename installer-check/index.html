What to do here

  1. 1

    Check.

    Type the installer’s name or website. You get their OneEthos standing and their Alliance standing on one card.

  2. 2

    Read the result.

    Approved by OneEthos and call logged means they are an Alliance installer and can use every partner’s terms. Anything else tells you what is still missing.

  3. 3

    Log your call.

    Talked to them? Log it below the result with your partner name and the date. One logged call from any partner is enough.

  4. 4

    They become an Alliance installer.

    The moment OneEthos approval and a logged call are both true, the status flips. When they take your offer, come back and log the enrollment so the other partners can see it.

Changes show here within 15 minutes. Anything you log is visible to every Alliance partner.

This information is confidential. It is for Alliance partner use only, and is not to be shared with the installer or with any third party. A result is a point in time and can change. Questions go to scott@bodhi.solar.
